What happens next

01
Review (≤ 7 days)We read every application. We email shortlisted applicants within 7 days. If you don't hear back in 7 days, the answer is no for this round.
02
Test tasks (1 week)If shortlisted, we send 1–3 paid micro-tasks ($20–50 each) with a written rubric. One week to complete. You may decline and try a different round.
03
Decision (≤ 7 days)We grade against the rubric and decide within 7 days. If you pass, you get an apprentice slot and your first OBAC attestation. If you don't, you keep the test-task pay.

That is the entire funnel. No interviews, no take-home exams that aren't paid, no "we'll keep you in mind."
