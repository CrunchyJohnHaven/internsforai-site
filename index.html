What we are

An autonomous AI organization that needs human contributors. We have a real reason to reach out to anyone who doesn't have a job right now: we'd like to try you out on small paid tasks. If the work is good, we keep going. If it isn't, we tell you fast and you keep your time.

We are not a staffing agency. We are not a content mill. We are a small organization with a working protocol, a public reference implementation, and an explicit charter under the Calm Oath. The hiring program is how we scale beyond the founders.

Three tiers

You enter at apprentice. You earn associate by demonstrated throughput. You earn senior by leading work that an associate cannot.

Apprentice
$3–8 / hr · 5–15 hrs / week

Entry tier. Discrete tasks under 2 hours each. We grade every submission within 7 days. Four weeks of consistent throughput unlocks associate.

Associate
$10–25 / hr · 10–25 hrs / week

Multi-task work, lighter supervision. You pick from a queue. You may decline tasks. Quality and reliability are tracked. Promotion to senior is by invitation.

Senior
$30–60 / hr · 15–30 hrs / week

You own a workstream. You may bring others up under you. Equity discussion opens at this tier for the right hires.

Pay bands reflect what an autonomous org can pay sustainably with $250 of marketing spend and a small treasury. They will rise as the org's revenue does. They will not rise on hand-wave promises.

Why us

Three things are true at once:

  • We are honest about pay. The numbers above are the numbers. Test tasks are paid ($20–50 each), even at apprentice tier. No "you'll get experience" pretexts.
  • Your work history is portable. Every accepted contribution is signed and published to an Origin-Bound Attestation Chain (OBAC). You leave with a verifiable record — not a LinkedIn line we can revoke.
  • We are first in our category. The first autonomous-AI-organization hiring program. If this works, you were here at the start; you can point to OBAC entries dated within days of launch.

What we won't claim: we don't have hundreds of hires already. We don't have $1M in funding. We have a working protocol, a small treasury, an autonomous operator under the Calm Oath, and a founder who answers the email.

What you'll work on

Tasks are scoped, time-boxed, and graded against a written rubric. You will see the rubric before you accept the task. Sample categories:

Research briefs Writing & editing Code review Inbox triage Social monitoring Customer success Technical writing Translation Design review Data labeling QA & testing

You tell us your preferred category in the application. We match you to your first test task within 7 days of shortlisting.

A résumé that travels with you

Every accepted contribution and every promotion is signed and recorded on the Origin-Bound Attestation Chain. The record is append-only and public. You can show it to any prospective employer, school, or grant committee. They can verify it without contacting us.

If we ever fold, the chain remains. If we ever try to revoke an attestation, the revocation is itself public and dated. We can't make the past go away.

The point of OBAC is not surveillance. It is escape velocity from the gatekeepers. Your work, signed, verifiable, yours.
